Compound Word Adventure

A fun, educational tool designed to help students learn and explore compound words. 
Built for teachers and parents to use alongside their students.

What It Does

Compound Word Adventure helps students recognize and understand compound words through interactive activities and guided exploration. 
Each compound word comes with a definition and an example sentence, so students can see the word used in context.

Features

• 📚 Compound words with clear, simple definitions
• ✏️ Example sentences showing each compound word in use
• 🌟 Designed to be easy for students to follow along

How to Use

1. Open the project
2. Follow the on-screen prompts to begin the activity
3. Work through the compound word challenges with your student

Who It’s For

• Teachers looking for a classroom activity
• Parents working with their kids at home
• Students learning about compound words for the first time

Built With

Python
